## Copy suggestions
### 1. Product description
**Original:** “Rill Pot. Ceramic. Diameter 13 cm. Matte green. A pot for indoor display.”

**Proposed wording:** “Rill Pot is a ceramic pot for indoor display, with a matte green finish and a diameter of 13 cm.”

Supported claims: F1. Proposal rationale: a connected sentence makes the same concrete details easier to read. Audience and suitability are unknown (F4); no beginner, plant compatibility, care or durability claim is included. No page-wide absence is alleged.

### 2. Collection introduction
**Original:** “Indoor display pots. Rill Pot, Brook Pot.”

**Proposed wording:** “Explore our indoor display pots: Rill Pot and Brook Pot.”

Supported claims: F2. Proposal rationale: an approachable introductory verb connects the supplied category label to its named products. This does not establish live availability, stock, full catalog membership or suitability. No page-wide absence is alleged.

Needs confirmation outside publishable copy: current product facts and collection names; audience and intended use before adding any fit language. Do not add free delivery, easy-care, beginner-friendly, durability or universal compatibility claims without supporting evidence.
